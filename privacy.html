Privacy Policy

Two Fs Make a D · Last updated 3 November 2026

The short version

The game does not collect, sell or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no ads and no tracking.

What stays on your device

The game saves a few things in your own browser so you can pick up where you left off:

  • your current game, best scores and today’s challenge progress
  • anything you type on the notes page

This never leaves your device. You can delete it by clearing this site’s data in your browser or by uninstalling the app.

Sharing your score

When you tap Share, the game writes a short message with your score. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you choose an app or paste it yourself.

Other services the game uses

  • Hosting: the game is hosted by Netlify, which may keep standard server logs such as IP addresses, as any website host does.
  • Fonts: the handwriting fonts load from Google Fonts, so Google may see your IP address when they load.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Questions

You can ask a question on the game’s GitHub page.
